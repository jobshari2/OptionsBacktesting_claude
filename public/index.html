        How to read these

        • 1 shows which strikes are getting the most new volume and whether it is calls or puts.
        • 2 shows when each strike got active. Brighter = more volume in that time bucket; the newest bucket is on the right.
        • 3 shows whether call or put activity is speeding up or fading.
        • 4 combines volume and price: big bubbles high up = heavy buying pushing the premium up; big bubbles below zero = heavy volume while the premium falls (likely writing).
        • 5 shows growth relative to each strike's own volume, so quieter strikes that suddenly wake up stand out.

            Upstox has no history of option prices, so every option is priced with Black-Scholes from the Nifty close and that day's India VIX (or the volatility you pick), with the skew, bid/ask spread and charges above.
