      ▾ ATM option charts

      ▾

      Major: clustered daily swing highs/lows (last ~120 sessions). Minor: 15-minute swings of the last 5 sessions, previous day's high/low/close and floor pivots. OI: strikes with the most open interest on this expiry. Levels below spot act as support, above as resistance.
      Nifty is an index with no volume, so the flow is read from the options within ±10 strikes of the money: each trade is classed as started by the buyer or the seller. Delta = calls bought + puts sold − calls sold − puts bought (in lots): positive leans up, negative down. Book lean is the share of the quantity waiting in the order book that is bullish (calls bid, puts offered). A divergence is price making a new high or low without the cumulative delta (CVD) following; absorption is heavy flow that does not move price. This is a read of the tape, not a prediction.
